About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Originally established as a centre for fisheries and marine science studies under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (now Universiti Putra Malaysia), it has grown independently into a premier focal point for marine science and aquatic resources research in the region.

UMT is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and community engagement. The university focuses strongly on harnessing the potential of marine science, oceanography, maritime technology, and related management disciplines to drive sustainable national development and global impact.
